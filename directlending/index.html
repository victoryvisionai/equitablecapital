Borrower Fit

Knowing quickly whether
this is worth a conversation.

Likely a fit
  • U.S.-based operating companies with revenue, cash flow, collateral, or a clear repayment source
  • Founders, CEOs, owners, or CFOs seeking $250K–$15M of near-term debt capital
  • Real estate operators pursuing acquisitions, value-add, refinancing, or bridge-to-permanent financing
  • Inventory-heavy consumer, distribution, wholesale, or trade-finance businesses
  • Lower middle-market PE firms or independent sponsors seeking portfolio-company financing
  • Multi-unit franchise operators needing growth, equipment, acquisition, or build-out capital
  • Founders or sponsors evaluating recapitalizations, partial exits, dividend recaps, or liquidity events requiring short-term bridge debt
Generally not a fit

To save time, these mandates are generally not intended for:

  • Pre-revenue or seed-stage startups
  • Pure equity capital needs
  • Borrowers without cash flow, collateral, or a credible repayment source
  • Capital needs below $250K
  • Long-dated financing needs beyond approximately three years
  • Highly speculative real estate or raw-land development without clear collateral and repayment support
  • Over-leveraged borrowers where total debt would exceed ~1× TTM EBITDA on a pro-forma basis
  • Non-U.S. borrowers, except select South American opportunities under Mandate A

If there is uncertainty, a brief conversation can usually determine whether the opportunity is worth reviewing.

How It Works

A straightforward path
from conversation to close.

Step 01
Initial Conversation

A 15–30 minute call to understand the borrower, use of proceeds, timing, collateral, and repayment source. No documentation required beforehand.

Step 02
Preliminary Fit Review

If there's potential fit, we request a focused package: recent financials, capital structure, use of proceeds, collateral summary, and transaction background.

Step 03
Lender Introduction

Equitable presents the opportunity to the appropriate lender through the GT Securities platform. The lender independently determines whether to proceed.

Step 04
Term Sheet & Closing

If the lender is interested, borrower and lender move through term sheet, diligence, documentation, and closing, with Equitable and GT Securities supporting the process.

There are no fees for the initial conversation or preliminary review. Any transaction fees would be addressed in a separate engagement letter.
