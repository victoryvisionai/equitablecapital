About Us

Equitable Capital Advisors

Equitable Capital Advisors is a relationship-driven capital markets and advisory firm built around a single operating principle: every node in the network gets value added, in whatever form fits them best, before revenue is ever discussed.

Andrew Glaze (West Point, Army veteran, Columbia MBA, ~10 years NASDAQ public board experience) operates through three platforms — GT Securities (260 bankers, $50B+ executed transactions, FINRA/SIPC member) for capital placement and M&A advisory; NFP/Aon for insurance and risk solutions referrals (Equitable is a Nevada-licensed insurance agency that refers all such work); and the AI Client & Partner Development Desk powered by Victory Vision for technology-enabled relationship intelligence and outreach. Equitable does not sell professional services off a price sheet. Target Audience

Who This Is For

We serve a two-sided network with role-specific engagement patterns.

Capital Deployers

Institutional direct lenders, private credit funds, family offices, emerging-manager-friendly FoFs, UHNW RIAs, multi-family offices with permanent capital, allocator-trustees at endowments. You consume opportunities, never pay for services. Revenue from this side comes from placement fees on what you fund through Equitable.

Capital Seekers

Emerging asset managers raising Fund I-III; founder-led operating businesses with capital event probability in 12-24 months; boutique founder-led service firms needing repeatable BD infrastructure; LMM borrowers $1-100M ticket; gov contractors; ecommerce inventory finance; specialty finance; franchise operators; CRE sponsors. Revenue from this side comes from placement retainers and per-transaction fees.

Channel Partners

Other senior bankers/advisors — coordinated referrals both directions. WP/vet-network bankers (AmeriVet, Jefferies, Cantor), boutique M&A shops, attorneys with HNW books. We build mutually beneficial referral relationships.

Dual-Role Contacts

Asset managers raising the next fund AND deploying current fund capital. Rare but valuable. We approach each conversation by asking what you need most right now; lead with that single tool; keep the rest in back pocket.

Process

How It Works

We lead with value, not pitch. Revenue follows when routed value compounds into a transaction or retained engagement.

1

Discovery

We identify what you need most right now — capital, partners, introductions, advisory, AI infrastructure, or a listening ear. Every conversation starts with understanding your specific situation and goals.

2

Value Routing

We deploy the right tool from our toolkit based on your needs. This might be a lender introduction, an LP placement, a strategic partner connection, or AI-powered outreach infrastructure — whatever moves you forward.

3

Engagement

When the routed value compounds into a transaction or retained relationship, we structure the appropriate engagement: placement fees, retainer mandates, M&A success fees, or AI desk retainers. Revenue is the result, not the starting point.
