Ideal Client Profile

Who This Is For

Equitable operates a two-sided network with role-specific engagement patterns. We serve capital deployers, capital seekers, and channel partners across the sub-institutional $1-100M ticket band.

Capital Deployers

Demand-side counterparties who consume opportunities. Revenue from this side comes from placement fees on what they fund through Equitable. We never pitch retainer services to this group.

  • Institutional direct lenders & private credit funds
  • Family offices & multi-family offices with permanent capital
  • Emerging-manager-friendly funds of funds
  • UHNW RIAs & allocator-trustees at endowments

Capital Seekers

Supply-side counterparties who need capital or business development. Revenue from this side comes from placement retainers and per-transaction fees.

  • Emerging asset managers raising Fund I-III
  • Founder-led operating businesses with capital event probability in 12-24 months
  • LMM borrowers $1-100M ticket (gov contractors, ecommerce, specialty finance, franchise, CRE)
  • Boutique founder-led service firms needing repeatable BD infrastructure

Channel Partners

Other senior bankers and advisors with whom we coordinate referrals in both directions. Mutual value exchange through network access.

  • West Point / veteran-network bankers (AmeriVet, Jefferies, Cantor)
  • Boutique M&A shops
  • Attorneys with HNW books

Dual-Role Contacts

Rare but high-value: asset managers who are both raising their next fund AND deploying current fund capital. We approach each conversation by asking what they need most right now.

  • Emerging managers with Fund II/III raising + active deployment
  • Family offices with both LP allocation and direct investment mandates
