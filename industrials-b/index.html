The Acquirer

The Buyer

This is a single-family office, not a private equity fund. The family built and operated a manufacturing business themselves. They understand what it takes to run a shop floor, manage a skilled workforce, and serve demanding customers in niche industrial markets.

They acquire roughly one company per year. This is not a roll-up strategy. This is not a platform play with aggressive add-on targets. Each acquisition is evaluated on its own merit and integrated with care.

Permanent capital. No fund life. No forced sale timeline. No clock ticking toward an exit. The family invests for the long term and measures success in decades, not quarters.

They do not pursue companies that are already private equity backed. They are looking for founder-owned or family-owned businesses where the current owner is ready to consider a transition on their own timeline.

What they look for:

  • $3-20M EBITDA
  • Niche manufacturing with genuine technical capability
  • Precision machining, metal forming and stamping, die sets and tooling, foundry work, aerospace and defense components, automation and machinery
  • Durable customer relationships and a reputation for quality
  • A business that would be difficult to replicate

What happens to the company:

  • Management stays. The people who know how to run the business continue to run it.
  • The workforce stays. No layoffs, no restructuring for the sake of efficiency gains.
  • The name stays. The brand and reputation you built remain intact.
  • The owner decides their own role and timeline. Stay involved as long as you want, step back when you are ready, or anything in between.
