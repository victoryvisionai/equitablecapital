Market Thesis

Why Southeast
multifamily.

A durable structural imbalance between housing demand and supply continues to favor well-located rental product in high-growth Southeastern markets.

  • Robust housing demand driven by Millennial and Gen Z populations, remote-work tailwinds, and steady household formation.
  • Long-term undersupply — an estimated 4.3M new apartment homes needed by 2035 to meet demand.
  • Shifting lifestyle trends — delayed marriage and delayed homeownership extend the renter lifecycle.
  • Growing renter preference among high-earning, white-collar residents.
  • Rising cost of homeownership — home prices, rates, and inflation pressure down-payment savings.
