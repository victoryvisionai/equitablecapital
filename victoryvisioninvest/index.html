About This Investment

A unique chance to invest in proven technology with demonstrated results

Victory Vision has built a proprietary AI platform that powers relationship intelligence, automated outreach, and client development for professional services firms, capital markets advisors, and emerging fund managers. The platform combines natural language processing, predictive analytics, and multi-channel engagement automation to deliver measurable revenue growth for clients.

This investment opportunity allows accredited investors to participate in Victory Vision's expansion into two high-growth verticals: AI-powered client development infrastructure for boutique financial services firms, and digital billboard networks that leverage the same AI targeting and content optimization technology.

Equitable Capital Advisors is sponsoring this raise and bringing the opportunity to a curated network of institutional allocators, family offices, and accredited individual investors. Andrew Glaze (West Point, Army veteran, Columbia MBA, ~10 years NASDAQ public board experience) operates Equitable through three platforms: GT Securities (260 bankers, $50B+ executed transactions, FINRA/SIPC member) for capital placement and M&A advisory; NFP/Aon for insurance and risk solutions referrals; and the AI Client & Partner Development Desk powered by Victory Vision.

The investment thesis is straightforward: Victory Vision has proven unit economics with existing clients paying $10-25K/month for AI-powered business development services. The capital raised will fund platform expansion, digital billboard infrastructure acquisition, and go-to-market acceleration across the target customer base of 5,000+ founder-led firms in Equitable's network.
