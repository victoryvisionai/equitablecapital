Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Victory Vision investment opportunity

What is the investment structure?

Victory Vision is raising growth capital through a private placement offering. Investment minimums, equity/debt structure, and expected return profile are detailed in the confidential private placement memorandum available to qualified investors after initial discovery conversation.

How does Victory Vision generate revenue?

Three revenue streams: (1) Digital billboard lease revenue from brand partnerships and programmatic ad sales; (2) AI Client Development Desk retainer subscriptions ($10-25K/month) from boutique founder-led firms and emerging managers; (3) Capital placement and M&A advisory fees through GT Securities partnership (retainer + success fees + tail provisions).

Who can invest in Victory Vision?

This offering is limited to accredited investors as defined under Regulation D of the Securities Act of 1933. Investors must meet income or net worth thresholds and complete subscription documentation including accreditation verification.

What is the use of proceeds?

Capital will fund: (1) Digital billboard infrastructure expansion (site acquisition, hardware, installation); (2) AI platform development (engineering talent, data infrastructure, feature roadmap); (3) Sales and marketing (client acquisition, channel partnerships, content creation); (4) Working capital and general corporate purposes.

What is the expected timeline to liquidity?

Victory Vision is building toward a strategic exit or IPO within 5-7 years. Interim liquidity may be available through secondary transactions subject to company approval. Investors should expect to hold their position for the full investment horizon and should not invest capital needed for near-term liquidity.
